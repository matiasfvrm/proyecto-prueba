#CH COLD OPEN
He has signed a massive NFL contract.
But he has also experienced the other side of professional sports.
He has been overlooked.
He has lost the biggest game of his career.
He has suffered multiple serious injuries.
And he has had to repeatedly prove that he belongs at the highest level.
That's why Joe Burrow's story isn't simply the story of a football superstar.
It's the story of what happens when success doesn't come easily.
Because sometimes the biggest part of a career isn't how many times you win.
It's what you do after you lose.
#CH CHAPTER ONE · ATHENS, OHIO
The story begins in Athens, a small college town in southeast Ohio.
Joe grew up around football. His father, Jim, spent decades as a coach.
Football wasn't just a game in that house. It was a way of life.
At Athens High School, Joe became the best player in the state.
In 2014, he was named Ohio's Mister Football.
And his dream was simple. To play for Ohio State.
#CH CHAPTER TWO · THE BACKUP
He got his wish. But the dream came with a waiting room.
In Columbus, Joe sat behind J.T. Barrett, one of the most productive quarterbacks in school history.
One season passed. Then another.
In 2017, he broke a bone in his throwing hand.
And when the starting job finally opened up, it went to someone else.
Three years. Zero starts.
Most players would have been crushed. Joe did something different.
He graduated early. And he left.
#CH CHAPTER THREE · BATON ROUGE
In 2018, he transferred to LSU.
A new team. A new city. A new chance to prove he was good enough.
He won the starting job, and LSU won ten games.
But nobody was ready for what happened next.
#CH CHAPTER FOUR · THE PERFECT SEASON
In 2019, LSU rebuilt its offense, and Joe Burrow became unstoppable.
Five thousand six hundred and seventy-one yards.
Sixty touchdown passes. Only six interceptions.
He won the Heisman Trophy by one of the largest margins in history.
And in his speech, he didn't talk about himself.
He talked about the kids back home in Athens who go to bed hungry.
Within days, people had donated hundreds of thousands of dollars to his hometown.
A few weeks later, LSU beat Clemson for the national championship.
Fifteen wins. Zero losses.
The backup from Ohio State had just played one of the greatest seasons college football had ever seen.
#CH CHAPTER FIVE · NUMBER ONE
In April 2020, the Cincinnati Bengals made him the first overall pick in the NFL Draft.
A team that hadn't won a playoff game in almost thirty years now had its franchise quarterback.
Then, in his tenth game, everything stopped.
His knee collapsed. A torn ACL. A torn MCL.
The rookie season was over.
And once again, people began to wonder.
#CH CHAPTER SIX · THE COMEBACK
Joe Burrow didn't wonder.
He came back the next season and played like he had never left.
He was sacked more than any quarterback in the league, and he kept getting up.
The Bengals won their division.
They won their first playoff game in thirty-one years.
In Kansas City, they came back from eighteen points down to beat the Chiefs in overtime.
And Joe Burrow was named Comeback Player of the Year.
#CH CHAPTER SEVEN · SO CLOSE
On February thirteenth, 2022, the Bengals played in the Super Bowl.
Late in the fourth quarter, they were ahead.
But the Rams scored with a minute and a half left.
Twenty-three to twenty.
Joe had been sacked seven times that night.
He had come within minutes of a championship.
#CH CHAPTER EIGHT · AGAIN
The next year, the Bengals returned to the AFC Championship Game.
This time, Kansas City won. Twenty-three to twenty. Another three-point loss.
In 2023, he signed the richest contract in NFL history at the time.
Two months later, he tore a ligament in his throwing wrist.
Another season, cut short.
So he rebuilt again.
In 2024, he led the entire league in passing yards and touchdown passes, and he was named Comeback Player of the Year for the second time.
#CH EPILOGUE
And then back into the fight once again.
His story isn't finished yet.
And perhaps that's what makes it so interesting.
Because Joe Burrow still has something left to prove.
Not to the people who doubted him.
Not to the people who questioned his injuries.
But to himself.
The journey from being overlooked to becoming an NFL superstar was already extraordinary.
Now, the question is what comes next.
